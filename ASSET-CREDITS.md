## MakeHuman

`assets/models/traveller-head.glb` is a derivative of the body mesh in MakeHuman's `makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`. The head was extracted, scaled, converted to GLB and fitted to the game's clothed characters. The mesh file explicitly states its release under CC0 in September 2020.

Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
License: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

Source copyright holders listed at the CC0 release: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. A copy of the CC0 text is in `assets/licenses/MAKEHUMAN-CC0.txt`.

This is an anatomical base, not a scan of the person in the user's reference. Skin appearance, eyes, hair, clothing and animation are implemented in this project.
