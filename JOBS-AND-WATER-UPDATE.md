# Jobs, shops and realistic water — what changed

## Kerala jobs
Six job sites are placed around the town; walk up to one on foot and press
**F** to get hired (no menu needed — one press clocks you in):

| Role | Where | How it pays |
|---|---|---|
| Supermarket Cashier | KURUKKANMOOLA SUPERMARKET counter | Stand at the counter; customers arrive every few seconds — press **F** to serve each one. |
| Meat Cutter | SREE MEAT STALL counter | Same counter loop, higher pay per customer. |
| Fish Seller | KADAL FISH MARKET counter | Same counter loop. |
| Shop Owner | KUTTETTAN STORES counter | Same counter loop. |
| Taxi Driver | TAXI STAND, next to the parked Kerala taxi | Get in the taxi (**E**) and drive to the gold pickup marker, then to the gold drop-off marker, for a fare. Repeats automatically. |
| Bus Driver | BUS DEPOT, next to the parked KSRTC/Swift/private bus | Same pickup → drop-off loop, driving any of the three buses. |

Money earned is shown top-right (**₹**) and is saved between sessions
(`localStorage`), same as your name and progress. Press **F** again at the
same counter/stand to clock out. The on-screen prompt at the bottom of the
screen tells you what pressing **F** will do at any moment — serve a waiting
customer, take a job, or clock out.

This is an arcade-style job loop (serve-the-customer / drive-the-fare), not a
scheduling or inventory simulation — it is meant to give you something to do
for money, not to model a real retail or transit business.

## Realistic waterfall and river
- The river/backwater/canal surface now moves with two layered swells plus a
  short choppy ripple and a visible downstream drift, instead of one small
  static wave term — it reads as flowing water rather than a flat tinted
  plane, especially from a distance or top-down.
- Kurinji Falls is now two cascading tiers separated by a rock ledge (with its
  own foam ring), flanked by cliff faces and boulders at the base, rather
  than a single flat sheet of falling water. Spray is spread across both
  tiers.

## Town dressing (also included)
Compound walls (now painted in varied colours, extended to shops), denser
roadside electric poles (some with transformers and working street lamps),
and more billboard variety/density — see `TOWN-REDESIGN.md` for the full
list from the previous pass, which is still in effect.
