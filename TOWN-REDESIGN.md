# Kurukkanmoola X Town Redesign

The town now has a stronger Kerala roadside identity:
- Compound walls and gate pillars around houses, villas **and shops**, painted from a
  six-colour palette so a street reads as individually owned homes rather than a
  repeated kit. Gateposts carry a small nameplate blank and a lamp finial.
- Wall coverage raised (from ~260 to up to 440 eligible properties) and the size/
  distance thresholds relaxed so more roadside plots get a compound wall.
- Concrete utility poles with crossarms, insulators and three overhead conductors,
  spaced closer together (65-85 m instead of 80-95 m) for a busier roadside look;
  cap raised from 120 to 210 poles.
- Every 4th pole now also carries a distribution transformer drum and a bracket
  street lamp with a real point light, wired into the existing day/night toggle.
- Roadside billboards: 16 fictional Kerala-style ad designs (bakery, tea shop,
  homestay, spice tours, jewellery, bus travels, Onam sale, ayurveda spa, etc.),
  placed more densely (every ~165 m instead of ~220 m) up to 80 boards.
- Utility/billboard/wall dressing remains static geometry merged with the town
  architecture, so none of this adds per-frame update cost.
- Existing roads, river, buildings, vehicles, UI, mountain viewpoint, wildlife and
  audio/performance fixes are retained.
