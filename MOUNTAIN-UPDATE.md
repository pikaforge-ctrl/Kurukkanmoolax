# Mountain Viewpoint Update

Added to the Kurukkanmoola X world:

- Mist/fog sprites concentrated around the Western Ghats mountain ridge.
- Nilgiri tahr, sambar deer, gaur, Asian elephant and leopard scenic encounters.
- Full timber watch tower at Churuli View Point.
- Additional wooden benches at viewpoints.
- Rustic Kerala-style hill tea shop with hanging banana bunches, counter/stools and a tea-shopkeeper NPC.
- Mountain grass is now static and restricted to the mountain zone, avoiding player-distance grass pop-in.
